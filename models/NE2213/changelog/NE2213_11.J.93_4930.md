### Luminous Rendering Engine

Adds seamless transition animations that create a more natural flow, with on-screen actions originating from and returning to their starting points within system apps.
Supports seamless drag-and-drop animations across Home screen icons, widgets, folders and Smart Sidebar for smooth, intuitive interaction.
Adds seamless transition animations when the text selection magnifier appears or disappears.
Adds seamless status bar transition animations when opening the notification drawer or Quick Settings. You can now interrupt the swipe-down gesture anytime with more seamless transitions.
Adds smooth transition animations when tapping the audio playback widget in Quick Settings for a more polished visual experience.
Supports parallel animations when launching and exiting apps from the Home screen in button navigation mode, delivering smoother transitions.
Optimises the exit animation curve for top-row app icons on the Home screen, delivering more precise control.
Optimises scrolling animation curves and touch responsiveness for the app list, delivering smoother, more responsive swiping.

### Trinity Engine

Trinity Engine delivers a smoother, more comprehensive experience. It dynamically adjusts system resources and processing loads to deliver sustained smooth performance – seamless short-video browsing, stable high-frame-rate gaming and uninterrupted photography sessions.
The system intelligently analyses key computing tasks during gameplay to ensure stable performance, while precisely managing concurrent operations like floating windows and video calls. Smart scheduling not only saves power but also delivers smooth, stable performance.
Faster photo album loading with lower power usage when loading thumbnails for longer battery life.
Improves the preview speed when viewing cached videos and thumbnails in some third-party apps for a smoother experience.
Optimises Power saving mode to adjust performance based on battery level, delivering longer battery life without compromising experience.

### AI-powered Photos

Photos gets a fresh new look and improved experience, with a redesigned photo view for smoother browsing with seamless transitions.
Optimises Photos interactions framework with clearer album categorisation and custom sorting for frequently used items, keeping everything neatly organised.
Redesigns the photo details page – swipe up to view photo info and storage location – now more intuitive and visually refined.
New video editing features, including clip cutting, merging, speed adjustment, transitions and background music. Pro-grade output, effortless editing.
Optimises video editing features, including customisable crop, rotation, parameter adjustment and filters.
Adds the motion photo collage feature, which preserves the original resolution and HDR effects of photos it uses. Plus more collage templates for greater personalisation.

### AI-powered Notes

Introduce a new content block edit mode – swipe content left or right to enter block editing mode. Easily select text, images, tables or documents with a single swipe for block formatting and quick rearrangement, faster than ever.
Adds a feature that allows you to touch and hold a photo to switch between views, along with a new side-by-side display, to improve your browsing experience.
Supports sharing notes via links. Now you can share text, images, audio and video content all at once, making it easier to share complete notes.
Supports text highlighting. Touch and hold to bold and recolour selections, streamlining note markup for efficient emphasis.
Adds multiple formatting features, including font editing, wavy underlines, dividers and text highlighting, to personalise your notes and enhance visual appeal.

### AI Recorder

The Recorder gets a complete makeover, with an optimised UI and smoother interactions.
Adds the smart naming feature, which automatically generates titles for recordings based on their content, making them easier to find and identify.
Adds AI-powered noise cancellation for human voices in recordings, significantly improving audio quality with clearer sound and more prominent voices.
New smart scenario detection capability automatically identifies recording contexts (such as meetings, lectures or interviews) and applies applicable templates for better transcripts.
Supports sharing recordings via links. You can share audio, transcript and summary together with a single tap.

Our new light field technology creates dynamic interplay between light and environment, mirroring light's movement and evolution for truly natural, captivating visuals.
Brings dynamic lighting effects into the system's design, elevating overall detail and texture.
Enhances Aurora visual effects, improves particle effects, glow effects, ripple effects, chromatic aberration and blending for a superior visual quality and premium interface experience.
Redesigns illustrations and diagrams for better aesthetics and readability.
Introduces the new system-wide serif font "One Serif", optimised for immersive reading with a classic, elegant aesthetic.
Introduces all-new weather visual effects – powered by Luminous Rendering Engine and real-time weather data to render realistic weather conditions with immersive effects.
Optimises the design and feel of core system interfaces like the notification drawer, Quick Settings, Setup Wizard and system upgrades for clearer information and a more refined look.
Redesigns interfaces for apps including Clock, Calculator and Compass with fresh aesthetics – now featuring real-world textures and lighting for a more delightful experience.
