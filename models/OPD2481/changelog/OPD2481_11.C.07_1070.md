## Luminous Rendering Engine
- Adds seamless transition animations when the text selection magnifier appears or disappears.
- Adds seamless status bar transition animations when opening the notification drawer or Quick Settings. You can now interrupt the swipe-down gesture anytime with more seamless transitions.
- Adds smooth transition animations when tapping the audio playback widget in Quick Settings for a more polished visual experience.
- New swipe-back animations. When swiping to go back in certain apps, you can now preview the previous page and cancel the action if needed. The animation follows your finger more naturally.
- New light field animations deliver more immersive and dynamic multimodal experiences powered by Luminous Rendering Engine.
- Optimises scrolling animation curves and touch responsiveness for the app list, delivering smoother, more responsive swiping.

## Trinity Engine
- Trinity Engine delivers a smoother, more comprehensive experience. It dynamically adjusts system resources and processing loads to deliver sustained smooth performance – seamless short-video browsing, stable high-frame-rate gaming and uninterrupted photography sessions.
- Improves storage reliability with optimised read/write performance, achieved through performance monitoring and system enhancements.
- Upgrades RAM compression by identifying and managing rarely accessed RAM. This ensures low-load conditions for apps, delivering smoother performance.
- Improves performance under heavy load, reducing background resource usage.
- The system intelligently analyses key computing tasks during gameplay to ensure stable performance, while precisely managing concurrent operations like floating windows and video calls. Smart scheduling not only saves power but also delivers smooth, stable performance.
- Boosts faster response across core system operations – quicker app launches and controls, plus smoother taps and swipes for an overall seamless experience.
- Optimises app launch speed for faster opening from the Home screen.
- Optimises Power saving mode to adjust performance based on battery level, delivering longer battery life without compromising experience.

## AI-powered Photos
- Optimises Photos interactions framework with clearer album categorisation and custom sorting for frequently used items, keeping everything neatly organised.
- Redesigns the photo details page – swipe up to view photo info and storage location – now more intuitive and visually refined.
- New video editing features, including clip cutting, merging, speed adjustment, transitions and background music. Pro-grade output, effortless editing.
- Optimises video editing features, including customisable crop, rotation, parameter adjustment and filters.

## AI-powered Notes
- Introduce a new content block edit mode – swipe content left or right to enter block editing mode. Easily select text, images, tables or documents with a single swipe for block formatting and quick rearrangement, faster than ever.
- Adds a feature that allows you to touch and hold a photo to switch between views, along with a new side-by-side display, to improve your browsing experience.
- Supports sharing notes via links. Now you can share text, images, audio and video content all at once, making it easier to share complete notes.
- Supports text highlighting. Touch and hold to bold and recolour selections, streamlining note markup for efficient emphasis.
- New shortcut format. When editing a note, you can type or tap the forward slash symbol (/) to bring up the formatting panel, reducing the need for frequent switching and improving editing efficiency.
- Adds multiple formatting features, including font editing, wavy underlines, dividers and text highlighting, to personalise your notes and enhance visual appeal.

## AI Recorder
- The Recorder gets a complete makeover, with an optimised UI and smoother interactions.

## New light field design
- Brings dynamic lighting effects into the system's design, elevating overall detail and texture.
- New light field animations deliver more immersive and dynamic multimodal experiences powered by Luminous Rendering Engine.
- Redesigns illustrations and diagrams for better aesthetics and readability.
- Introduces the new system-wide serif font "One Serif", optimised for immersive reading with a classic, elegant aesthetic.
- Introduces all-new weather visual effects – powered by Luminous Rendering Engine and real-time weather data to render realistic weather conditions with immersive effects.

## Flux Home screen
- New Home screen icons featuring light field-enhanced design language, with richer details and more realistic visuals.
- Optimises Home screen layout grid and elements. Icons, folders and widgets now display more elegantly and are more organised.
- Supports touching and holding large folders on the Home screen to change the layout. Choose layout styles like nine-grid view, four-grid view or large icon highlighting the first app.
- Introduces the textless Home screen for a cleaner, more refined look.
- Improves Home screen editing mode with page reordering support.
- Increases the maximum number of apps that can be added to the Home screen's bottom dock area.
- Redesigns Drawer mode pages with the search bar moved to the bottom of the Home screen for easier app search.
- The new Drawer mode allows you to swipe the index bar to quickly select apps.
- Adds categorised app view in Drawer mode – apps can now be grouped by category for easier access.
- Improves app recommendations in Drawer mode, featuring frequently used and recently used apps.
- Redesigns the layout and style of the Home screen, Lock screen and style page with more intuitive categorisation and better layout.

## AI-powered Flux themes
- Introduces a new modular editing framework with real-time preview for greater editing control.
- Adds custom Lock screen text. Personalise your Lock screen with any message you like.
- Adds support for customising quick-access shortcuts in the bottom left-/right-hand corners of the Lock screen for faster access to frequently used features.
- New flux theme icon styles adapt to your wallpaper colours with multiple customisable styles for perfect visual harmony.
- Introduces new Dark mode icon styles for a more eye-friendly and comfortable experience.

## O+ Connect
- Redesigns the cross-device connectivity page layout in system settings, now organised by device type and usage scenarios for clearer categorisation and easier access.

## Smart Cars
- Upgrades Bluetooth connectivity with in-car devices for a better user experience.

## My Devices
- The new My Devices feature, accessible from Quick Settings, allows you to view and manage your connected devices.
- Adds support for My Devices widgets on your Home screen. You can add widgets for selected devices or an overview of all devices for easier management.

## Network optimisation
- Optimises the AI LinkBoost network kernel scheduling algorithm to improve network fluency for popular apps.

## Notifications & Quick Settings
- Supports more actions when editing Quick Settings tiles. More tiles now support the 2×1 size. Supports removing, adding or rearranging audio player, brightness slider, volume controls and My Devices modules.
- Redesigns notifications with a more compact layout for better browsing efficiency.

## More details
- Adds the option of playing ringtones only through earbuds. When this option is turned on, incoming calls will only play through connected earbuds, helping maintain quiet in public spaces.
- Adds system language support for Bosnian.
- Redesigns Smart Sidebar with refreshed visual styling, now supporting single/dual-column display.
- Redesigns the share panel layout to show contacts directly and allow editing app shortcuts for faster sharing.
- Streamlines banner notifications – menu buttons now open apps in floating windows instead of full screen for a cleaner experience.
- Upgrades the battery page with improved data visualisation. Now shows foreground/background activity times for more accurate power usage statistics.
- Upgrades data transfer speed and experience when migrating data from iOS to OnePlus devices.
