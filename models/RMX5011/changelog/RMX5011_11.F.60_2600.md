### Effortless AI at every moment

AI Gaming Coach now supports a floating crosshair for quick aiming, improving your shooting stability and accuracy.
Introduces the AI Edit Genie feature, which supports both AI LightMe and AI StyleMe, allowing you to choose your favourite lighting effects from various templates and create unique styles with exclusive filters.
Introduces the AI FastCut feature, which intelligently recommends various templates for you to choose from, enabling you to create captivating short videos with just one tap.
Adds the PowerPoint translation feature, which supports translating presentations into multiple languages, preserving original formatting, viewing original and translated text side-by-side, and exporting translated files.
Improves the AI Eraser feature by making images clearer and reducing visible traces in removed areas for a more natural effect.
Improves the Remove reflections feature by eliminating glare from document photos and portraits of people wearing glasses.

### Smart lifestyle from every detail

Adds a "Popout" option to the "Collage" feature that allows the photo subject to "pop" out of the frame, adding a fun factor to photo editing.
The torch now automatically remembers its last brightness setting, so you don't have to adjust it next time you use it.
Timers now display estimated stop times, making them easier to use.

### Network connection

Adds iPhone connectivity. When your phone is connected to an iPhone and they are near each other, you can view the iPhone's incoming calls, SMS messages and notifications on your phone.
Adds the "Automatically open received files" switch, which lets you choose whether to automatically open shared files upon receipt.
Adds a new interface for AI Netpilot Network Engine, where you can view the number of network optimisations, reduction in lag time and more.
