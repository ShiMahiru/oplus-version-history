## Down to the last detail
- You can now connect your device to two sets of Bluetooth earphones simultaneously, each with its own adjustable volume, making it easy to share audio with someone else.
- The Weather app now shows moonrise, moonset, and lunar phases on its homepage, keeping you informed about the current lunar cycle.
- The app drawer's Categories view now features an "App suggestions" section, which smartly highlights your most-used apps for faster access.

## Home screen & Lock screen
- Improves transition animations when opening and switching apps in "Recent Tasks," resulting in a faster, more fluid multitasking experience.

## Device Connect
- You can now lock your PC remotely with a single tap. After your tablet connects to your PC remotely, you can unlock your PC directly from the tablet without entering your PC's password.

## Security & privacy
- You can now add a backup face for device unlocking, so you can access your device even if you're wearing glasses or makeup. You can find this feature in "Settings - Security & privacy - Device unlock - Face & fingerprint unlock - Face - Add backup face".
- You can now turn off "Accessibility" permissions with a single tap. If apps installed from unofficial sources attempt risky actions, a pop-up will alert you and allow you to quickly revoke the relevant permission.
- Integrates the July 2026 Android security patch to enhance system security.
