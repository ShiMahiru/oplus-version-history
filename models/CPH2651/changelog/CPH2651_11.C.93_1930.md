Adds the AI-powered menu translation feature. Instantly generates dish images, allergens, and ingredients while translating. Also recognizes prices, converts currencies, and adds items to cart for seamless overseas dining.

### AI-powered Documents

Adds an auto-save feature for scans, saving scanned documents to Documents for easy access and management (Documents - Recents).

### Live Space

Improves the Lock screen with a dual-mode design featuring a notification capsule that displays notifications and Live Updates without covering the wallpaper. View key information at a glance in immersive mode without unlocking your device.

Supports sorting Home screen apps by color or category with one tap for a cleaner layout.

Improves network switching speed and accuracy for better connectivity in low-signal areas such as basements and elevators.
