# CPH2813_16.0.10.500(EX01)

> 官方更新日志 · 来源: OTA descriptionInfo
> OTA 指纹: `CPH2813_11.A.40_0400_202608071408`

## Home screen & Lock screen
- You can now clear all app notification badges on the Home screen by spreading two fingers on the Home screen.

## System tools
- You can now draw directly on images in Notes to highlight key points.

## Camera & Photos
- Introduces the AI Remix Collage feature, allowing you to extract subjects from Videos and save them as animated stickers. You can apply these animated stickers to Videos, motion photos or still Photos to create new motion photos, or add animated outlines for more creative designs.
- Fixes an issue where some Camera icons, text and menus might mistakenly display in portrait mode while in landscape mode.

## Security and privacy
- Improves virus protection by automatically blocking high-risk behaviours, such as the abuse of Accessibility services, logging detected events and providing alerts to help protect your data. You can find this feature in "Phone Manager - Viruses & risks - Block suspicious app activities".
- Improves how you use hidden apps. While using a hidden app, swipe up from the bottom of the screen toward the floating window area in the upper-right corner to switch it to a floating window.
- Integrates the August 2026 Android security patch to enhance system security.
