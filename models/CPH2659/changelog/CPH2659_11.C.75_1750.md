### Luminous Rendering Engine

Improves animations in Livestream Assistant when launching, resizing, moving or switching between floating and full-screen windows for more responsive controls.
Improves pull-down animations when swiping down from the status bar to open the notification drawer or Quick Settings.

### Trinity Engine

Improves system stability with AI-powered touch recognition for smoother swipes in apps.

### AI Translate

Adds the AI-powered menu translation feature. Instantly generates dish images, allergens and ingredients while translating. Also recognises prices, converts currencies and adds items to basket for seamless overseas dining.
Adds a new on-device AI model in Translate for high-quality translations even without an internet connection.
Adds AI-powered summaries for real-time interpretation, generating automatic titles and summaries for translated conversations.
Improves real-time interpretation with context awareness for more accuracy.

### AI-powered Photos

Adds a more customisable Popout option in Collage with flexible popout directions and subjects.

### AI-powered Documents

Adds an auto-save feature for scans, saving scanned documents to Documents for easy access and management (Documents - Recents).
Speeds up document scanning for faster continuous multi-page capture.
Improves document scanning with enhanced algorithms that remove wrinkles, shadows and blur for clearer digital documents.
Improves text scanning to recognise handwriting, paragraphs and tables, with support for copying content or exporting it to Word documents.

### Live Space

Adds support for swiping down to tuck Lock screen notifications and Live Alerts into capsules to reduce clutter, with swipe-up expansion and smooth animations.
Adds the timer focus mode on the Lock screen. Tap the timer in Live Alerts to view your countdown in full screen without unlocking.
Adds new Live Alerts swipe animations on the Lock screen. Swipe the Live Space for smoother, more dynamic transitions.

### Flux Home screen

Supports sorting Home screen apps by colour or category with one tap for a cleaner layout.
Adds the auto-save feature for Home screen layouts, allowing one-tap restore.
Adds support for finding apps by colour in Drawer mode.

### Upgrades light field design

Adds "Contour glow" to bring richer, more organic textural details to system interfaces like Home screen, Lock screen, Quick Settings, Global Search, Quick Glance and Photos, following real-world light behaviour.
Adds HDRUI display technology, delivering higher dynamic range in colour and brightness for the large Lock screen clock.

### Camera

Redesigns Camera with cleaner UIs and more natural interactions, enhancing the shooting experience.
Introduces Master presets. You can now save your custom parameters as presets and share them via watermarks.

### More

Improves power consumption statistics at the app level with tracking for personal hotspot usage and activity duration.
Integrates the May 2026 Android security patch to enhance system security.
