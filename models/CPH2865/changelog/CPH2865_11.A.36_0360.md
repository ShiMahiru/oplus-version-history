### Home screen & Lock screen

You can now choose from more custom fingerprint icons and animations. You can find this feature in "Settings - Home screen, Lock screen & style - More - Fingerprint animation".
Improves transition animations for launching and switching apps in "Recent Tasks", delivering smoother, more responsive operation throughout multitasking.

### Camera & Photos

Adds the "Primrose" filter, which produces warm, delicate skin tones in portraits and effortlessly evokes a vintage film aesthetic.
Adds the "Bold B&W" filter, specifically designed for documentary photography. Its striking high-contrast light and shadow layers create a powerful visual impact.

### Games

You can now use the equaliser to adjust game sound effects, creating your own sound style and experiencing gaming audio tailored to your preferences. You can find this feature in "Game Assistant - Tools - Sound boost - Equaliser".

### Device Connect

You can now lock your PC remotely with one tap. Once your phone is connected to your PC remotely, you can unlock it directly on your phone without entering your PC's password.

### System tools

Improves gesture navigation display by hiding the gesture guide bar by default. You can find this option in "Settings - System & update - System navigation - Gestures - Hide gesture guide bar".
