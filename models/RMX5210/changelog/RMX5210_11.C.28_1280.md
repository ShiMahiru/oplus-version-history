### Fluid design. Smooth in motion, vivid by nature.

- Introduces an all-new fluid design, crafted for seamless interactions. Smooth in motion, vivid by nature. With seamlessly integrated state transitions, gentle motion rhythms, and lively feedback, the fluid design makes every interaction feel natural.
- Adds responsive light effects that balance readability, transparency, and visual aesthetics in a lightweight, friendly, rounded design.
- Adds fluid animations. Screen and element transitions now feature fluid merging effects, creating seamless connections between elements and more natural transitions between states.
- Adds a flexible feedback system that enables interface elements to naturally stretch, compress under pressure, and rebound dynamically in response to user interactions, while light effects actively respond to user intent and interface changes to convey status, feedback, and guidance through light, making every interaction feel more responsive and creating a more natural, vivid experience.
- Introduces the next-generation Luminous Rendering Engine with a new fused rendering architecture that renders light effects and the user interface together, delivering greater rendering efficiency and more refined, seamless fluid animations and visuals.
- Adds a fluid clock effect that seamlessly carries the clock across the Always-On Display, Lock screen, and Home screen, creating a natural, cohesive unlocking experience.
- Improves the Lock screen swipe-up unlock animation. App icons now animate into view with the fluid clock as a visual anchor, creating a seamless transition from the Lock screen to the Home screen.
- Adds a downward collapse effect for Lock screen notifications, allowing notification cards to stack naturally, and improves the progressive transitions and swipe-rebound animations for notification display changes.
- Adds responsive behavior to the Lock screen clock font, dynamically adjusting its size based on notification content while ensuring readability at the minimum size.
- Improves the visuals and animations of Lock screen widgets, bottom shortcuts, and Live Space capsules, with a new flexible feedback system applied to these elements.
- Improves the Lock screen password keypad visuals by optimizing the progressive entry animation and introducing a flexible feedback system for more responsive interactions.
- Introduces a new Quick Settings design with responsive light effects for tap interactions, providing more vivid feedback, and improves the visuals and animations on certain secondary pages.
- Redesigns the notification drawer with a lighter, more translucent card design, enhancing information contrast and readability. Adds responsive light effects for tap interactions, making the feedback more vivid.
- Improves the transition animation between the notification drawer and Quick Settings by introducing a sequential motion effect and dynamically adjusting element movement based on gesture focus and swipe speed, making transitions more responsive, natural, and fluid.
- The Sublime Grandeur theme wallpapers now display real-time local weather, so you can check the latest weather information directly from the Home screen and Lock screen.
- Adds Lock screen style switching for Flux themes, allowing you to freely switch between clock, graphic-and-text, and photo frame styles to easily create a personalized Lock screen (available for select Flux themes).
- Adds ColorOS 17 fluid wallpapers, with unlock animations that respond to the rhythm of your gestures in real time, making wallpaper switching more natural and device unlocking more immersive.
- Adds rotation animations for apps running in landscape mode to make app launch and exit transitions more natural, and improves switching between landscape and portrait modes when a mini window is present on the screen for a more seamless experience.
- Refreshes Live Alerts with light, translucent visuals inspired by clouds. Responsive light effects flow naturally with your gestures, while clear, precise tap feedback makes interactions more intuitive and refined.
- Adds responsive light effects to Live Alerts for alarms, countdowns, timers, realme Share, and other scenarios, with refined animation details.
- Improves music Live Alerts with responsive light effects, including light effects that guide playback progress and diffusing ripple effects when switching songs, making playback feedback more dynamic and immersive while providing a consistent visual experience across the Lock screen and Quick Settings.
- Introduces a brand-new design language to system apps, refreshing their look, and improves the designs of the Settings, System & update, About device, and other screens.
- Introduces a new system-wide floating-island navigation design for a more immersive app experience, with fluid merging effects when switching between navigation items for more seamless connections between elements.

### Laminar Flow Engine

- Upgrades Laminar Flow Engine with a new context-aware scheduling system, optimizing memory management to effectively reduce foreground lag under high memory load and prevent unnecessary background app closures.
- Introduces smarter background app management that uses on-device personalized modeling to better understand your intentions and keep the right apps running in the background more intelligently.

### Mind Space

- Adds the Travel collection, which automatically organizes your trip locations into a personalized travel map.
- Improves memory collections to generate structured image-and-text summaries, making key information easier to browse and understand at a glance.

### Photos

- Improves the Memories feature in Photos by using on-device AI models to intelligently understand the relationships between people, events, and time in photos, automatically selecting more story-rich photos and creating personalized memory albums.
- You can now view photos and videos from certain third-party devices directly in Photos, and browse and preview them without importing them to your device, making cross-device content viewing more convenient.
- You can now select photos and videos directly from certain third-party devices in third-party apps, and share or edit them with a single tap without first saving them locally, making cross-device creation more efficient.

### OSee

- Introduces OSee, which integrates the system's AI vision capabilities. Take a photo, and it will intelligently understand your intent and recommend relevant features.
- Adds ultra-clear document scanning, enabling you to quickly scan paper documents and generate electronic documents with one tap, making it easier to save and manage your documents. The feature also supports ultra-fast continuous scanning with no waiting between consecutive scans. The all-new AI Hyper Clarity algorithm intelligently optimizes text, edges, and image quality to deliver clearer, sharper document scans.
- Supports one-tap, layout-aware document extraction that intelligently preserves headings, paragraphs, tables, and other formatting elements, quickly converting paper documents into editable electronic documents.
- Supports scanning and exporting to PDF, as well as merging multiple documents. Merge multiple scanned files with one tap for more efficient document organization.
- Supports intelligent recognition of various QR code content across system services, third-party apps, and webpages. Scan to pay, add contacts, open services, and more with a single tap.
- Integrates the system's Snap & Translate feature. AI intelligently understands text in images and provides accurate translations in a single shot, making it easy to read menus, road signs, manuals, and other foreign-language content.

### AI Translate

- Introduces an all-new Translate app with an AI-enhanced translation experience and refreshed interface design. The three core capabilities—photo translation, voice translation, and text translation—are now directly accessible from the bottom navigation, making it easier to switch between them.
- Supports more languages for AI model-powered translation (Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Vietnamese, Thai, German, French, and Indonesian), delivering more natural translations.
- Improves the real-time simultaneous translation experience with faster, smoother responses. Streaming speech recognition and incremental translation work together to achieve ultra-low latency. Large language models use contextual reasoning to accurately handle long sentences, colloquial speech, and professional terms, while voiceprint recognition and speaker separation clearly distinguish between different speakers.
- Improves voice translation when using earphones, supporting voice-cloned playback to make communication during travel, business, and multi-person meetings more seamless and efficient.
- Improves voice translation when using earphones, making translation faster and more accurate.
- You can now triple-tap or pinch supported earbuds to start voice translation without taking out your phone. The bone-conduction sensor works with the multi-microphone array to accurately identify different speakers, making multi-person simultaneous translation more accurate and natural.

### Device Connect

Once paired, your camera is automatically detected when powered on and placed near your phone or tablet, and connects with a single tap through Live Alerts, making cross-device connectivity more effortless.
