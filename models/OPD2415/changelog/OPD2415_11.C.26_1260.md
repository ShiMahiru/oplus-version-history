### Luminous Rendering Engine

Smoother, more responsive animations now cover all floating window actions in Livestream Assistant, from launch to resize to full-screen toggle.
Split View interactions are smoother than ever. Edge dragging feels more responsive, and a refined bottom layer adds clearer visual depth.
Swiping down from the status bar to open the notification drawer or Quick Settings now feels smoother, with more polished pull-down animations.

### Trinity Engine

AI-reconstructed touch recognition eliminates scrolling jitter inside apps for a noticeably smoother experience.

### AI Translate

The Translate app now includes a dedicated on-device AI model, delivering natural, accurate translations even when you're offline.
Live translation sessions now generate automatic titles and AI summaries, making it easy to review and organize translated conversations afterward.
Live translation now takes context into account to deliver more precise, natural-sounding results in real time.

### AI Recorder

The new AI Recording Sticker feature in Recorder lets you tag important moments with text or images, whether while recording or during playback.

### AI-powered Documents

Documents now supports multiple windows open at once, making it easy to cross-reference files and multitask efficiently.
Scanned documents are auto-saved to your Documents app right after capture, keeping everything organized without extra steps (Documents - Recents).
Document scanning is now faster, making back-to-back multi-page captures quicker and more efficient.
Upgraded scanning algorithms remove wrinkles, shadows, and blur in one tap, producing sharp, clean digital documents.
Enhanced text scanning now recognizes handwriting, full paragraphs, and tables, with easy one-tap copy or Word document export.

### Live Space

Improves the Lock screen with a dual-mode design featuring a notification capsule that displays notifications and Live Updates without covering the wallpaper. View key information at a glance in immersive mode without unlocking your device.
Swipe down to collapse Lock screen notifications and Live Alerts into tidy capsules, and swipe back up to expand the full list. Smooth animations make the whole interaction feel effortless.
Stay on top of your timers without unlocking: Tap a timer in Live Alert to expand your countdown to full screen instantly.
Switching between Live Space capsules is now smoother and more dynamic with fluid animations.

### Flux Home screen

Organize your Home screen instantly by color or category with a single tap.
Home screen layouts are auto-saved as you go, so you can revert to any earlier setup with just one tap.
Find apps by color in Drawer mode for quicker navigation.
Set distinct Home screen arrangements for portrait and landscape orientations, with icons, folders, and widgets each snapping to their designated layout whenever you rotate your device.

### O+ Connect

Screen Collaborate turns your tablet into a secondary display for your PC, with extended and mirror modes for flexible dual-screen setups.
When using your tablet as a second screen for your PC, the stylus now unlocks handwritten annotations on computer files, precise editing, and pressure-sensitive drawing.
One keyboard and mouse, two devices. Keyboard & Mouse Share seamlessly connects your PC and tablet. Move the cursor to the edge to switch devices, and drag files or share your clipboard across screens.

### Free-Flow Window

Free-Flow Window arrives with full multi-window support: drag edges or corners to resize each floating window exactly as you like.
Use four-finger pinch and spread gestures to switch between floating windows and Split View.
Multitasking just got a serious upgrade. Run three apps side by side, drag windows to floating mode, and resize Split Views by pulling the edges.
Improved floating window management keeps your view clear: windows hug the sides when you swipe to the Home screen, and adapt their size and position when full-screen apps open.
Floating windows in Recents now display in a tiled layout and automatically adjust their position and size for a cleaner overview.

### More

My Files now works with external keyboard shortcuts like Copy, Paste, and Select all, plus drag-to-select for mice and touchpads.
Battery stats become more informative with new coverage of personal hotspot usage and activity time at the app level.
The title bars in Notes and My Files have been reorganized with a dedicated tools area for faster, more convenient access to common actions.
Introduces quick file access, which allows you to create file shortcuts on the Home screen, drag files to the task bar, and add file shortcuts to the sidebar in My Files. When an external storage device is connected, its files can also appear directly on the task bar, making file access faster and everyday use more convenient.
File Explorer has been upgraded with multi-window support, versatile display layouts, and hover previews for a desktop-class browsing experience.
Improves computer-style file management with drag and drop support. Easily move files and folders to the Home screen, task bar, sidebar, or some third-party apps.
Integrates the May 2026 Android security patch to enhance system security.
